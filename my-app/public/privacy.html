The short version

Pomodoro has no account, advertising, or behavioural analytics. Your timer settings, optional task labels, and focus history are handled locally on your device. The developer does not receive this focus content.

01

Data on your device

Pomodoro stores timer state, timer preferences, optional task labels, and completed-session history locally so that the timer and offline features work. This content is not sent to Jojo Moustak.

02

When the app goes online

When an internet connection is available, Pomodoro may request the current web files from Vercel, its hosting provider. To deliver and protect the service, Vercel may process technical request information such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser or device details, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic logs.

Pomodoro does not add advertising or behavioural analytics code, and Jojo Moustak does not use hosting request information to profile you. Vercel handles that information under its Privacy Notice.

03

Permissions

The app may use these Android capabilities only for its core features:

  • Notifications — active-timer and session-completion alerts.
  • Alarms and reminders — a completion alert at the expected time.
  • Vibration — optional completion and interface feedback.
  • Network access — checks for and loads the current web version when online.

Pomodoro does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

04

Sharing and retention

Jojo Moustak does not sell personal information and does not share your local timer, task-label, or focus-history content with third parties. Local data remains on your device until you remove it using available in-app controls or Android's app-storage controls.

Technical information processed by Vercel follows Vercel's own security and retention practices.

Pomodoro keeps local focus content inside the app's Android storage and uses HTTPS for hosted web requests. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

05

Your choices

  • Delete completed-session history from the History screen.
  • Change sound and haptic preferences inside Pomodoro.
  • Manage notification permission and notification-channel sound in Android settings.
  • Manage all locally stored app data through Android's app-storage controls.
